My HTML Notes

<h1> </h1>
Creates headings on the page, it has 6 levels of importance (h1, h2, h3, h4, h5 and h6), with h1 being the main one.
<strong> </strong>
Emphasizes the content, displaying it in bold typography
<q> </q>
Represents a quotation, and adds quotation marks
<hr>
Displays a line dividing the page, and represents a change in subject
<table> </table>
Creates a table layout and the content between the opening and closing tags, will be formatted as a table (as long as proper tags are used along the way)
