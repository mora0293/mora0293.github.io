My HTML Notes

<h> </h>
Creates headings to the page, it has 6 levels of importance (h1,h2, h3, h4, h5 and h6), h1 being the main one.
<strong> </strong>
Emphasizes the content, dysplaying in a bold typography
<q> </q>
Represents a quotation, and adds quotation marks
<hr>
Displays a line dividing the page, and represents a change on the subject
<table> </table>
Creates a table layout and the content between the opening and closing tags, will be formated as a table (as long proper tags are being used along the way)
